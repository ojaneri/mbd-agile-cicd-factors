## Lead check against the manuscript and testbed (2026-11-14)
All 17 quotations match main.tex. 14 points correct as stated. Three correct in substance with a suggested
wording that was not taken as written:
- Point 4 suggested "real automotive-class microcontroller": the STM32L4 (and the nRF52840) are general-purpose
  MCUs (also VR5-K-07). Used "a general-purpose Arm Cortex-M4 board".
- Point 11 suggested "the model … driven by recorded inputs": MIL is driven by the requirement test vectors;
  recorded data feed only the field oracle. Written accordingly.
- Point 1: "Automotive" holds for S1 and S2; S3 is a stationary storage battery used only in the confirmatory
  experiment. Title adopted at the owner's instruction ("Yes, apply it" to the plan).
- Point 16 partly answered already by the Fig. 1 caption; "cumulative" was not explained.
- Point 15: the generator is the project's own script (`testbed/*/mutate.py`), model-level operators on the
  spec (model and code regenerated by `gen.py`), code-level operators on the generated C; all 298 mutants
  compiled ("live" meant "not stillborn" and was dropped as a term). Counts per class from
  `testbed/{soc,fuel,bess}/results/e1_qemu.jsonl`.
- Point 17: DeMillo et al. 1978 and Jia & Harman 2011 verified on Crossref (DOIs in refs.bib).
- Found while checking: r5's "is implemented twice" (d-0023) was wrong; `gen.py` generates both model and C
  from one spec. Corrected.
