# Abstract (draft, working title)

status: draft v0, for viability only

Working title: Integrating Model-Based Design, Agile, and CI/CD for Safe Software-Defined Vehicle Development: A Comparative Framework

Argument:
1. MBD gives early, rigorous validation (MIL/SIL/HIL/VIL) but high upfront cost and weak change flow.
2. Agile gives flexibility and feedback but is informal for safety evidence.
3. CI/CD automates integration, testing and OTA release but needs toolchain and governance.
4. The paper compares the three on target, artifacts, validation, time to deliver, change management and tooling, then proposes a four-stage flow (model and design, plan and develop, integrate and test, release and deploy) with feedback loops.
5. Claims to validate: [[claims/c1-integration-gain]], [[claims/c2-standards-traceability]].

Gap: the comparison is qualitative in most sources; no quantified case for SDV with standards traceability.
